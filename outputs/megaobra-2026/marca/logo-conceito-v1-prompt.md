## Prompt final

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: one initial logo signature concept for a Brazilian B2B marketplace, “Mercado Geotécnico”, to review before adoption.
Primary request: create ONE original, polished, flat logo with an original compact geometric symbol on the left and a highly legible wordmark on the right. The marketplace serves companies advertising geotechnical and foundation parts, equipment and specialist services. Convey reliable industrial expertise and commercial connection.
Symbol: simple strong silhouette, subtle abstract reference to connected subsurface soil layers and foundations, using a few bold interlocking geometric bands and a small orange connecting accent. It must remain identifiable when the symbol alone is used in a small circular social avatar. Do not enclose the full logo in a circle. Avoid literal illustrations.
Wordmark text (verbatim, and the ONLY text): “Mercado Geotécnico”. The exact accent in “Geotécnico” is mandatory. Spell Geotécnico as G-e-o-t-é-c-n-i-c-o. Arrange “Mercado” and “Geotécnico” in two neatly aligned lines beside the symbol. Use confident contemporary geometric sans-serif typography resembling Geist Bold, with excellent kerning and clear readability.
Color palette: preserve brand blue #0f4c81, dark navy #0a2540 and accent orange #f97316. Keep orange restrained. No other colors, apart from antialiasing. Opaque solid colors within the logo.
Composition: a single centered horizontal logo lockup on a landscape transparent canvas, clean balanced proportions, generous transparent clear space, all lettering and symbol fully within the image.
Background: genuinely transparent alpha background, not white and not a drawn checkerboard.
Style: sober premium industrial B2B identity; vector-friendly flat geometry, crisp smooth edges.
Avoid: hard hats, buildings, generic gear/cog icons, excavator illustrations, gradients, shadows, 3D, mockups, textures, photos, slogans, extra words, watermark, multiple variants or a presentation board.
```
